# Design — Soualhi Dental

- Direction (Taste Skill draw, seed 1666056799): "The place carries it" — one full-bleed photograph of the room fills the first screen; the name rides a rail across the midline that docks as the header on scroll.
- Idea: the bright Casablanca room itself, open on the Atlantic, becomes the argument; the live thing is the before/after smile slider the visitor drags.
- Palette redraw (recorded): the dealt graphite/orange palette fights the subject's own material, so proportions are kept (one ground, one ink, one accent, alternating fields) and colours come from the cabinet: bright white ground #FBFDFD, deep marine ink #0A2E3F, azure accent #0E7C9B, deep azure field #0B3A4D. WhatsApp green appears only on WhatsApp actions.
- Type: Zodiak (100/300, display) + Hanken Grotesk (400/500, text), five steps only.
- Motion (Emil): custom ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1), UI under 300ms, buttons scale 0.97 on press, popovers origin-aware, transform/opacity only, slow Ken Burns drift on the hero photograph, staggered section entrances, full prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
- bans respected: no gradients, no eyebrows, no index numbers, no hairlines outside forms/chat, no shadows at rest, square image corners, pill buttons everywhere.
